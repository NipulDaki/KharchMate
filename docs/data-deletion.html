Data Deletion Policy & Request

Updated September 2026
100% Offline-First Architecture: KharchMate operates completely offline. All your data (expenses, incomes, budgets, custom categories, and profile) is stored exclusively on your physical device inside a secure SQLite database. We do not transmit, back up, or host your personal financial data on remote cloud servers.

1. Overview

At KharchMate, we believe you should have complete ownership and control over your personal financial data. You do not need to create an online account or register credentials to use the app.

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data and Data Deletion Policy, this page explains how you can immediately wipe your data from within the app, through device settings, or by submitting a formal deletion request to our developer team.

2. In-App Data Deletion (Instant)

You can permanently delete all your financial records and profile data directly inside the application at any time:

  • 1
    Open the KharchMate app on your device.
  • 2
    Tap the Settings tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  • 3
    Scroll down and select "Delete My Data" (with the red trash icon).
  • 4
    A confirmation dialog will appear explaining the permanent action. Tap "Delete Data" to confirm.

Result: All transactions, monthly budgets, custom categories, and personal profile information (name, email, currency settings) are instantly and permanently erased from the local database. Default categories and initial onboarding state are cleanly restored.

3. Deletion via Device Settings or Uninstall

Because all data lives exclusively on your phone's internal storage, you can also erase all data without opening the app:

Clear App Storage (Android): Go to Settings → Apps → KharchMate → Storage & cache → Clear Storage. This wipes all database files and settings immediately.
Uninstall Application (Android & iOS): Long-press the KharchMate icon and select Uninstall / Delete App. When uninstalled, iOS and Android automatically delete the app's entire sandbox and local SQLite database files with zero recovery possible.

4. Web / Email Data Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled KharchMate or cannot access your device and want to confirm or submit a formal data deletion inquiry, you can email our support team directly:

  • 1
    Send an email to manavinfotech8@gmail.com with the subject line KharchMate — Data Deletion Request.
  • 2
    Specify the name or contact email entered during your profile setup (if any) and details of your request.
  • 3
    Our team will review your inquiry within 24 to 48 hours and send you written confirmation that no cloud data, server accounts, or personal records exist on our systems.

6. Third-Party Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

To support free development, KharchMate displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may process device identifiers and anonymous usage data in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

You can reset or permanently delete your mobile advertising identifier at any time through your device settings:

Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (turn off tracking for apps).
