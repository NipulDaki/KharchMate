Help & Support

Find quick answers to common questions about tracking your expenses, managing categories, and keeping your finances organized with KharchMate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new expense or income?
▾
  • 1
    Tap the "+" (Plus) button located in the center of the bottom navigation bar.
  • 2
    Choose whether the entry is an Expense or Income using the toggle at the top.
  • 3
    Enter the Amount in your chosen currency.
  • 4
    Select a Category (e.g., Food, Shopping, Salary, Bills).
  • 5
    Select your Payment Mode (Cash, UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking).
  • 6
    Pick the Date & Time (defaults to now) and optionally add a descriptive Note.
  • 7
    Tap "Save Transaction" — your balance and statistics update immediately!
How do I edit or delete an existing transaction?
▾
To Edit a Transaction:
  • 1
    Go to the Transactions tab from the bottom navigation (or tap any transaction under Recent Transactions on Dashboard).
  • 2
    Tap on the transaction to open the Transaction Details screen.
  • 3
    Tap the "Edit" button at the bottom.
  • 4
    Update amount, category, payment mode, date, or note, then tap "Save Changes".
To Delete a Transaction:
  • 1
    Open the Transaction Details screen as described above.
  • 2
    Tap the red "Delete Transaction" button at the bottom.
  • 3
    Confirm in the prompt to permanently remove it from your records.
How do I create and manage custom categories?
▾

You can manage and create custom categories in two ways:

  • From Settings: Go to Settings → Categories, switch between Expense and Income tabs, and tap "+ Add Category".
  • While Adding a Transaction: In the Add Transaction screen, scroll to the end of the categories list and tap "+ Add".

You can pick a custom name, choose from a rich library of category icons, and select an accent color. Custom categories can be edited or deleted anytime from the Categories page.

Does KharchMate work offline without internet?
▾

Yes! KharchMate is completely offline-first. All your expenses, incomes, budgets, categories, and settings are stored locally on your physical device using a fast, embedded SQLite database. No internet or mobile data is ever required to log or manage your transactions.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?
▾

No. KharchMate works immediately with zero signup, passwords, or cloud accounts. Your name and email address entered during onboarding are kept strictly on your local device solely for personalized display inside the app.

How do I change my currency?
▾
  • 1
    Go to the Settings tab in the bottom navigation.
  • 2
    Tap on Currency.
  • 3
    Select your preferred currency (e.g., INR ₹, USD $, EUR €, GBP £, CAD C$, AUD A$, AED د.إ, JPY ¥).
  • 4
    All amounts, balances, and reports across KharchMate immediately update to your selected currency symbol.
Where can I view my spending reports and trends?
▾

Tap the "Reports" tab in the bottom navigation bar. Here you can view:

  • Your total income, total expenses, and net savings for any month.
  • Visual category spending breakdown with percentage shares.
  • Top spending categories ranked by total amount.
  • Month-over-month financial trends.
What happens if I uninstall the app or clear app data?
▾

Because KharchMate stores all your data 100% locally on your physical device for privacy, deleting the app or clearing its storage in device settings will permanently erase your local database. We recommend noting down your monthly summaries before resetting your device.

Is my financial data private and secure?
▾

Yes, 100%. Your financial records are never uploaded or synced to external servers, nor are they sold to any third party. Everything is kept confidential on your device. Please read our Privacy Policy for full details.
